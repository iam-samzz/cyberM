What is Capture The Flag in Cybersecurity?

In cybersecurity education, a Capture The Flag (CTF) is a gamified simulation where learners solve technical security puzzles to discover a hidden secret token known as a "flag". Flags are placed inside deliberately vulnerable web applications, encrypted communications, network packet streams, or compiled program binaries. Finding and submitting the flag proves the participant has successfully identified and understood the underlying security flaw.

Jeopardy-Style Challenges

Challenges will span distinct domains like Web Exploitation, Cryptography, Forensics, and Reverse Engineering, each offering graduated difficulty levels from beginner to advanced.

Adversarial & Analytical Thinking

CTFs train students to think like both ethical attackers and forensic defenders—analyzing unintended system behaviors, breaking weak controls, and dissecting anomalous payloads.
